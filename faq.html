A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Frequently asked
questions.

Find quick answers to some of the things people ask us about life at SkyNest.

What is co-living?

Co-living is a style of accommodation where residents have their own private room or space while sharing selected facilities and social areas with others.

Who can live at SkyNest?

SkyNest is designed for young professionals, students, digital nomads and others looking for a convenient shared-living environment.

Are the rooms furnished?

Our accommodation options are planned around furnished living. Contact the team to confirm the features included in a specific room.

Can I choose a private room?

Private room options may be available depending on the package and current availability. Get in touch to discuss your preference.

Can I arrange a viewing?

Yes. Use the Contact page to enquire about arranging a viewing and our team can help with the next steps.

Are short-term stays available?

A Short-Term Stay option is included among our packages. Contact us to discuss suitable dates and availability.

How do I book a room?

Choose a package and select its booking enquiry button, or contact our team directly. We can guide you through the process.

Can I request a custom package?

Yes. Tell us your preferred room type, length of stay and requirements so the team can discuss a suitable option.

What facilities are available?

Facilities may include co-working spaces, Wi-Fi, shared kitchens, laundry, a rooftop garden, fitness and wellness spaces, lounges and study areas.

Is Wi-Fi available?

High-speed Wi-Fi is one of the facilities planned for residents. Ask the team to confirm current arrangements and coverage.

Can residents use the kitchen?

Shared kitchen facilities are part of the SkyNest co-living concept. Residents should follow house guidelines and keep shared areas tidy.

Is laundry available?

Laundry facilities are included in the planned resident amenities. Contact SkyNest for details about access and usage.

Are there spaces to work or study?

Yes. Co-working spaces and quiet study areas are intended to support work, study and focused tasks.

Are social events organised?

Community events are part of the SkyNest experience, giving residents opportunities to meet people and take part in activities.

Is there a gym or wellness area?

Fitness and yoga or wellness spaces are included in the planned facilities. Ask which amenities are available at your chosen location.

Can guests visit?

Guest arrangements may depend on residence rules. Please contact the team to confirm the current guest policy.

How much does a stay cost?

Prices can depend on the package, room type and length of stay. Contact SkyNest for current rates and a personalised quotation.

What is included in the package?

Each package has different inclusions. Review the Packages page and contact the team to confirm exactly what is included.

How long can I stay?

Stay arrangements depend on the package and availability. Short-term and longer-stay enquiries can be discussed with the team.

Are bills included?

Utility and bill arrangements have not been specified here. Please ask the team for a full breakdown of costs and inclusions.

What payment methods are accepted?

Payment methods and schedules should be confirmed directly with SkyNest before making a booking or payment.

Is the building secure?

Security is an important part of the facilities plan, including 24/7 security arrangements. Ask for details about the property.

How can I contact SkyNest?

Visit the Contact page to find the contact details and send an enquiry to the SkyNest team.

What if I have another question?

Use the feedback and question form below. Share your details and the team can follow up with you.
